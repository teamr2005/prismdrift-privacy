Prism Drift – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Prism Drift (“the game”, “we”) is a free Android game published by Wj Games. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner handle.

1. Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Your progress (shards, upgrades, skins, settings) is stored only in a save file on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use:

  • Advertising ID (and, where permitted, device identifiers)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language)
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (ad views, clicks, crash and performance information)

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google’s own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive this data and do not sell personal information.

3. Consent and your choices

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where required see a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice later in the game under Settings → AD PRIVACY when that button is shown.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads (for example a second chance or double shards) are always optional.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

5. Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data. Your local save file is deleted when you uninstall the game or use Settings → Reset Progress. For data held by Google, see Google’s privacy policy and your Google account’s ad settings.

6. Security

The game does not transmit your save data anywhere. Ad requests are sent by the Google SDK over encrypted connections.

7. Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change (for example a new SDK), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
